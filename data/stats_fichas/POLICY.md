# Statistics fichas: publication policy

## Decision

- **Date:** 28-oct-2026
- **Decided by:** Dirección, approving the proposal "Fichas estadísticas por familia"
- **Decisions:**
  1. **One live ficha per family.** It is replaced each period, and the previous one goes to `archive/`. It replaces the loose SIS minimal cards of the series that belong to the family.
  2. **Auto-publish on PASS.** PASS means deterministic verification plus reconciliation. On FAIL the ficha goes to HOLD.
  3. **The LLM writes only the "Lectura Alicanto".** It is limited to 90 words and may use only numbers that are already in the evidence package. With no budget, or when the verifier fails, the block is omitted and the ficha still publishes.
  4. **Build order.** F1 Cartera, then F2 Financieras, then F4 GES, then F3 Licencias.

## Scope

- **Covered:** the families marked `"status": "active"` in `config/stats_fichas_v1.json`. Today that is F1 only.
  - Files: `data/stats_fichas/live/<slug>.json`, generated by `scripts/run_stats_fichas.py` from `src/radar_salud/stats_fichas.py` and `src/radar_salud/stats_ficha_<family>.py`.
- **Not covered:** newsletter, payments, other AI features, and radar candidates. They still follow CLAUDE.md item 3.
  - A planned family is not activated by this policy. Activating it requires its builder, tests and a PR to staging.
- **F2 (FEFI):** no auto-publication while `HOLD_COVERAGE_ESENCIAL` is active (see `config/editorial_rules_v3.json`).

## Conditions to auto-publish (all are required)

1. **Reconciliation PASS**, recorded in `reconciliation` in the evidence package. For F1 that means:
   - the 10 official codes are present each month;
   - cotizantes + cargas = beneficiarios for every Isapre;
   - the sum across Isapres equals the declared "Total Sistema";
   - the months are contiguous;
   - the declared period equals the last month in the file;
   - the release has a single hash.
2. **Deterministic verification PASS** (`verify_ficha`):
   - every number shown in the headline, dek, KPIs, charts, tables, facts, watch items and notes traces to the package;
   - every chart row points to a package value;
   - every source has an https URL and a SHA-256;
   - headline ≤ 140 characters, dek ≤ 40 words, 3–5 KPIs, 2 charts.
3. **A trigger fires** (`evaluate_triggers`):
   - a new period or release;
   - a revision of more than 0.5%;
   - a late release, which adds an item to "Qué mirar".
   - A threshold crossing marks the ficha as featured but does not by itself republish.

## HOLD (never publishes)

- **Triggers:**
  - reconciliation failure;
  - missing hash;
  - a period older than the live ficha;
  - a deterministic verification FAIL.
- **What happens:**
  - The live ficha stays as it is.
  - The attempt is written to `hold/<slug>-<period>.json` for the coordinator.

## Lectura Alicanto (model)

- **Budget:**
  - one call with the fast route (`luna`) per new release;
  - worst case ≤ USD 0.05 per run;
  - the shared counters in `ai_budget`, the paced monthly budget (`paced_budget_usd`) and the weekly cap enforced by `openai_runtime.call_json`.
- **Verifier** (`verify_lectura`), which rejects:
  - **any number at all** (`forbid_numbers`, review FAIL of 28-oct): digits, any Unicode numeric character and number words, fractions, multiples and ordinals (`number_words`: veinte, mil, millones, doble, triple, mitad, tercio, cuarto, segundo…). The KPI strip and "Qué muestran los datos" already carry every figure with its unit, sign and period, so the Lectura adds implication only; this closes every unit, sign, year and bag-of-values bypass by construction;
  - causal wording without a causal note (`causal_patterns`, regular expressions over the folded text that cover conjugations: impuls…, origin…, responde a(l), provoc…, gener…, deb… a, explic…, caus…, atribu…, se traduce, lleva a, produce, porque…);
  - any insurer (full catalog with aliases, Fonasa included) that is not in the package **and** named in "Qué muestran los datos";
  - more than 90 words, and URLs.
  - After the Lectura is attached, the whole ficha is verified again (repeated sentences, audience); a failure drops the Lectura and the ficha still publishes.
- **No repeat payment:** a paid attempt for the same `release_key` is never repeated automatically. Only `not_attempted` and `no_budget` are retried.

## Web

- `scripts/export_web_snapshot.py` emits each live ficha as a feed card (`stats_ficha`). It removes:
  - the legacy "Pulso Isapre" card;
  - the loose cartera, regional cartera, suscripciones and movilidad cards whose release the ficha covers: monthly cards with a period ≤ the ficha's data period, and month-less yearly books published on or before the ficha's release (the #84 rule). A newer release stays visible until the ficha updates.
- `web/stats-ficha.js` renders the ficha with a "Compartir informe" action. That action shares `data/fichas/<slug>.html` (OG preview, `twitter:card` summary), which redirects to `index.html?abrir=1#sig-…` and opens the ficha detail. It uses the Web Share API, falls back to copying the link, and sends no email.

## Corrections

- A withdrawn finding keeps its text verbatim in `superseded_meta` (`withdrawn_insight_texts`), with `corrected_at`, `reason` and the superseded and correcting parser versions (CLAUDE.md rule 6). The card shows a short dated note ("Corregido el 28-oct: …").
