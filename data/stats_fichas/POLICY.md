# Statistics fichas: publication policy

How to build a ficha, and where each design and truth rule is enforced, is in [PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md) (in Spanish).

## Decision

- **Date:** 28-oct-2026
- **Decided by:** Dirección, approving the proposal "Fichas estadísticas por familia"
- **Decisions:**
  1. **One live ficha per family.** It is replaced each period, and the previous one goes to `archive/`. It replaces the loose SIS minimal cards of the series that belong to the family.
  2. **Auto-publish on PASS.** PASS means deterministic verification plus reconciliation. On FAIL the ficha goes to HOLD.
  3. **The LLM writes only the "Lectura Alicanto".** It is limited to 60 words. With no budget, or when the verifier fails, the block is omitted and the ficha still publishes.
     - **Figure rule, changed 28-oct-2026 15:25:** "Lectura Alicanto puede tener cifras, solo que no caiga en ser redundantes, debe aportar algo más." This replaces the no-numbers rule of the 28-oct review.
  4. **Build order.** F1 Cartera, then F2 Financieras, then F4 GES, then F3 Licencias.

## Conditions to auto-publish (all are required)

1. **Reconciliation PASS**, recorded in `reconciliation` in the evidence package. For F1 that means:
   - the 10 official codes are present each month;
   - cotizantes + cargas = beneficiarios for every Isapre;
   - the sum across Isapres equals the declared "Total Sistema";
   - the months are contiguous;
   - the declared period equals the last month in the file;
   - the release has a single hash.
   - **Year on year (F1 v10, 4-nov-2026):** the same month one year earlier, only from the previous-year SIS monthly
     workbook captured by the cartera lane (seed `cartera_release_prior` in `config/source_expansion_runtime.json`, same
     fetcher, robots.txt and host allowlist; `market_sources.collect_prior_portfolio` →
     `data/market/isapre_portfolio_<year>.json`). It must pass the same checks (one hash, 10 codes, cotizantes + cargas,
     Σ = Total Sistema, months of that year only). Otherwise the ficha says "a/a pendiente"; the base is never estimated.
   For **F4 GES** (measure decided by Dirección on 3-nov-2026, "Ene–jun vs ene–jun"):
   - the parser `ges_ytd_cuts_v5` (`src/radar_salud/ges_workbook.py`): the sheets of the reporting year, the previous year and the one before are pinned by name; each sheet has a two-row header (column groups over FONASA/ISAPRE); in every column read the problem rows add up to the declared total, Fonasa and Isapre;
   - table structure (review of PR #129): problems numbered 1…n with no gap or repeat, n ≥ `F4.capture.min_problems` (80); 'Subtotal' rows are skipped; the 'Total' row is the last labelled row (a coded or labelled row after it, e.g. an intermediate 'Total Decreto…', HOLDs `HOLD_GES_ROWS_AFTER_TOTAL`);
   - the cumulative cuts are stored as evidence of kind `cumulative_input`: never in the pool of showable numbers, never compared as revisions;
   - **first publication gate** (`F4.first_pass_requires_review`): until a year-to-date F4 ficha is live, a PASS is written as HOLD `awaiting_first_review` with the full rendered ficha in `hold/f4_ges-<period>.json`; it publishes only when `F4.first_review` records `reviewed_by` and `reviewed_sha256` equal to that release's workbook SHA-256;
   - the measure is year-to-date: cases that entered between January and the month of the cut (the SIS column "Ingresos entre Ene y <mes> <año>"), compared with the same months of the previous year. It is confirmed in the registry (`F4.measure.confirmed`, `confirmed_by`); without it HOLD (`measure_unconfirmed`). The header can only veto: a measure column saying "desde 2005", a quarter or both keeps the HOLD;
   - the SIS cumulative counts since July 2005 ("Número de casos acumulados Jul-2005 a …") are never shown as a headline, KPI or any figure; they are only differenced;
   - year-to-date identity, per problem and in total, per insurer: the year-to-date column = cumulative(t) − cumulative(December of the previous year), for the reporting year and for the previous year's December column; else HOLD (`HOLD_GES_YTD_IDENTITY`);
   - the same months of the previous year = cumulative(t − 12 months) − cumulative(December two years before), from the same workbook; quarters (trend) = `cut(t) − cut(t−1)`, and Q1 = the March cut − the December cut of the previous year (never the March cut alone);
   - cumulative cuts never decrease (December of the previous year ≤ March ≤ … ≤ t);
   - a capture parsed by an older GES parser HOLDs (`parser_outdated:…:recheck_pending`) until the daily capture re-reads the same file once with the current parser; the bytes must hash to the recorded SHA-256 (else `hash_mismatch`).
   For **F6 Boletín IP**:
   - one PDF per topic (acreditación, mediación, reclamos, RNPI), whose file names carry the release's own period. A release page that links another release's PDFs (the "Septiembre 2026" page, 28 oct) is rejected, never relabelled;
   - every figure comes from the independent reading (`config/statistical_analysis_verified_v1.json`) and is found in the text of the captured, hashed PDF, on a page the reading cites (`pages_1based`) and next to a context word of its metric ("acreditad", "trámite", "mediador", "reclamo", "títulos"…);
   - privadas + públicas = solicitudes en tramitación; con observaciones ≤ acreditados; the recomputed claims change rounds to the change the SIS reports.
2. **Deterministic verification PASS** (`verify_ficha`):
   - every number shown in the headline, dek, KPIs, charts, tables, facts, watch items and notes traces to the package;
   - every chart row points to a package value;
   - every source has an https URL and a SHA-256;
   - headline ≤ 140 characters, dek ≤ 40 words, 3–5 KPIs, 2 charts.
3. **A trigger fires** (`evaluate_triggers`):
   - a new period or release;
   - a revision of more than 0.5%;
   - a late release, which adds an item to "Qué mirar".
   - A threshold crossing marks the ficha as featured but does not by itself republish.

## F4 and F6 rules

- **F4 rates** are "tasa bruta": year-to-date cases of an insurer / mean beneficiaries of the same insurer over the month-end stocks of the same months × 1.000. They count cases, not persons (one person can have more than one GES case). The Isapre denominator is the F1 capture. Fonasa beneficiaries are not captured, so the Fonasa rate is "tasa no disponible"; it is never estimated.
- **F4 decree changes** are marked from the data: a problem (by GES number) present in only one of the two periods compared is flagged, kept out of the ranking, and the total change is also shown for the comparable problems only. Official decree notes go in `F4.decree_changes` only with a source.
- **F4 top problems:** a year-on-year change of 15 % or more, over a base of at least 500 cases in the same months one year earlier.
- **F4 supersedes** the loose GES card (`stat_families: ges`) only while the F4 ficha is live in PASS; until then that card stays in HOLD.
- **F6 stocks** (acreditados, solicitudes en tramitación, mediadores, títulos) are never summed across releases. Quarter-on-quarter comparisons need two verified releases and stay "pendiente" otherwise. RNPI counts titles, not unique professionals.
- **F6 new releases (Dirección, 1-nov-2026, Boletín N°3-2026):**
  - A release may also arrive as loose PDFs of the Intendencia de Prestadores listing (one history record per PDF, no release page). One PDF per topic with the same number and period in the file names forms the release (`stats_capture.loose_boletin_releases`). It is captured with the same fetcher and limits and dated by first detection, never as a publication.
  - Checker v3 (acreditación): a metric with an approved figure template is placed only by that template, which must match exactly once on the cited pages (the cut-off date in the template is variable). Without an independent reading, the capture records a template pre-check for the reviewer; it never verifies a figure. A reading added later re-checks the document once.
  - Until the new release passes, the ficha keeps the last verified release and the new one is written to `hold/<slug>-<period>.json` (`release_holds`).
  - Meanwhile its loose PDF cards are shown as **one** card titled from the documents ("Boletín Estadístico Informativo IP N°3 · enero–septiembre 2026", `sis_bulletins.collapse_loose_bulletins`). It lists the PDFs, says the figures are being verified and links the live ficha; it shows no figure. Once the ficha covers that release, the card is absorbed like any loose card of the family (`stat_period` read from the file names).

- **F6 topics and N°3 (Dirección, 2-nov-2026):**
  - Topics: Acreditación, Con observaciones, Reclamos, Mediación. The RNPI PDF stays in the release check (captured, hashed, its reading verified), but no RNPI topic, figure or source is shown.
  - Headline generated from verified values: "Prestadores acreditados llegan a 1.009 a septiembre, 38 más que en junio; 124 con observaciones" (wording by the sign of the verified change; the level only without a verified previous bulletin).
  - "Mostrar con nota": a figure whose source table does not add up is shown only with the reading's short footnote (`source_notes` in `config/statistical_analysis_verified_v1.json`, placeholders filled from that document's verified figures). SIS numbers are never corrected; a note naming an unknown figure HOLDs (`source_note_invalid`).
  - Every parse records the hash of the reading it checked (`reading_sha256`, all checker versions). A parse whose reading was added or edited after it, or that carries no hash (parses from before 2-nov), HOLDs its release (`reading_awaits_document_recheck`) until the capture run re-checks the hashed PDF once against the current reading. A release whose previous bulletin is in that state also HOLDs (`previous_release_reading_awaits_document_recheck`), so it is never published without its comparison. A HOLD leaves the live ficha as it is.
  - Quarter-on-quarter comparisons only for acreditación stocks, whose definition both committed texts confirm; mediación shows its level only. A figure shown with a note (e.g. solicitudes en tramitación) appears only in the chart that carries the note, never in the dek or topic text.

## Text receipts

- **Purpose:** the reviewer who writes the independent reading of a new release (e.g. Boletín IP N°3-2026, period 2026-09: acreditación, mediación, reclamos, RNPI) reads the documents' text from the repository; only the daily run can download SIS files. A receipt is reference text for that reviewer. It never verifies a figure: figures are still checked against the hashed document (`parse_boletin_pdf`).
- **Basis:** the documents are public statistics bulletins and spreadsheets published by a government body, the Superintendencia de Salud, on its own site. Keeping their extracted text next to the URL and SHA-256 is a citation receipt of public official information. Receipts are written only for URLs on the SIS allowlist (`www.superdesalud.gob.cl` / `superdesalud.gob.cl`, HTTPS, default port, no credentials). The text of any non-government, private, media or paywalled source is never stored.
- **Content:** `url`, `sha256`, `bytes`, `format`, `extracted_at`, `extractor` (pypdf or openpyxl, with version) and, per page, `text_raw` (as extracted) and `text_normalized` (`stats_capture.normalize_page`, the normalization of the figure templates). For xlsx: `sheet_names` and the first 40 rows of each sheet (cells tab-separated, one row per line).
- **Limits:** at most 60 pages (sheets) and 200 KB of JSON per document; past a cap the receipt says `truncated` and why (`max_units`, `max_bytes`).
- **Once per sha256:** a receipt that exists for the same SHA-256 is never rewritten.
- **Backfill:** a captured document of the newest release of each kind (F4 GES, F6 Boletín) without a receipt is downloaded once more for it, after captures and re-checks, with the same fetcher, limits and per-run download budget (6 per kind). The bytes must hash to the recorded SHA-256, else nothing is written (`text_receipt.status: hash_mismatch`) and it is not retried. A failed download is retried 3 times, then weekly. The capture record (status, parse) is not changed. Older releases are not backfilled.
- **Commit:** `data/stats_fichas` is already committed by the daily workflow; no workflow change.

## HOLD (never publishes)

- **Triggers:**
  - reconciliation failure;
  - missing hash;
  - a period older than the live ficha;
  - a deterministic verification FAIL.
- **What happens:**
  - The live ficha stays as it is.
  - The attempt is written to `hold/<slug>-<period>.json` for the coordinator.

## Lectura Alicanto (model)

- **Budget:**
  - one call with the fast route (`luna`) per new release;
  - worst case ≤ USD 0.05 per run;
  - the shared counters in `ai_budget`, the paced monthly budget (`paced_budget_usd`) and the weekly cap enforced by `openai_runtime.call_json`.
- **Verifier** (`verify_lectura`, parser in `src/radar_salud/lectura_verifier.py`). Third independent review, 28-oct, closed by class:
  - **Insurers.** Names, aliases and misspellings are found first (exact names; `CB`, `BMI` only in upper case; then edit distance ≤ 2 on names longer than 5 letters with the same first letter, common words excluded) and masked before any number is read, so "Vida 3" and "Vida Tres" are one insurer and the "3" is never a figure. Fonasa includes "seguro público" and "aseguradora estatal". Any insurer outside the package fails (`insurer_not_in_package`), and one in the package but not in "Qué muestran los datos" fails too (`insurer_not_in_facts`).
  - **Parse.** Each figure becomes (value, decimals, comparator, sign, unit, subject, period). The unit is the metric noun after the figure, or before it in the same clause ("los cotizantes subieron en 1.296"); "N … por cada …" is a ratio; "7 Isapres" is a count.
  - **Scope.** Every `lectura_pool`, `derived_ratios` and `ranks` entry stores `scope` (`system` / `open` / `closed` / `insurer:<code>`). A figure is credited to the insurer or group phrase ("el sistema", "las Isapres", "las abiertas", "las cerradas") that the clause names; with none named, to the system. A system figure credited to an insurer fails (`number_entity_mismatch`).
  - **Period.** Every entry stores `period_kind` (`month` / `ytd` / `interval` / `stock_at`). A month named in the clause matches only `month` or `stock_at` entries of that month. `ytd` requires YTD wording ("desde enero", "en lo que va del año", "entre enero y agosto"). `interval` takes no single month.
  - **Comparators.** These are checked against the true value:
    - "más de", "superior a", "sobre": true > stated;
    - "menos de", "inferior a", "bajo": true < stated;
    - these bounds must also be within 10 %, so "más de 1" never passes;
    - "casi": true < stated and within 5 %;
    - "cerca de", "alrededor de", "unos", "aproximadamente": within 5 %.
  - **Number words.** As before, they must equal a `derived_ratios` or `ranks` entry. An ordinal must trace to a rank of the ranking it names (size, decline, growth, streak) and that ranking's period. It is bound to an insurer only through a copula ("Colmena es la segunda").
  - **Adds something.** A sentence with figures must cite a traced id outside D that is not `trivial`; entity counts such as the 7 open Isapres or the 3 that grow are trivial. Every sentence, with or without figures, fails as a restatement when its content-word Jaccard against a displayed sentence is ≥ 0.5 (`restates_shown_text`), even if it carries a new id. A sentence without figures must also name an audience from `implication_terms`, the single audience list used everywhere.
  - **Also rejects** causal wording without a causal note: the earlier list plus "por lo que", "así que", "de modo que", "de manera que", "lo que hizo que", "lo que llevó a", "nace de", "viene de", "proviene de", "en razón de", "por ende", "en consecuencia". It also rejects more than 60 words, and URLs including bare domains (`superdesalud.gob.cl`).
  - After the Lectura is attached, the whole ficha is verified again. A failure drops the Lectura and the ficha still publishes.
- **Second-model review** (`review_lectura`, `with_review`). After the deterministic verifier passes, one reviewer call checks the Lectura against the evidence package and returns PASS/FAIL with the claim it contests.
  - Route: `lectura.review_route`, `luna`, the cheapest route, with 200 output tokens.
  - Budget: the same budget machinery. `max_run_usd` covers writer + reviewer, so a draft that cannot be reviewed is never paid.
  - Limit: at most 1 reviewer call per ficha. A text that already passed review is never reviewed again.
  - FAIL, no budget, a reviewer error, or no model in the run: the Lectura is omitted (`review_fail` / `review_no_budget` / `review_error` / `review_not_run`) and the ficha still publishes.
- **Chart headlines** derive their counts from the package. "tres crecen" comes from `conteo.abiertas_crecen`. A count word that matches no derived count fails `data:chart_headline_count_not_in_evidence`.
- **Prompt** (`lectura_prompt`): lists what is already shown and, separately, the "Material nuevo" (pool entries, ratios and ranks outside D); the Lectura must add an implication, comparison or derived figure from that material and never restate what is shown.
- **No repeat payment:** a paid attempt for the same `release_key` is never repeated automatically. Only `not_attempted` and `no_budget` are retried.

## Web

- `scripts/export_web_snapshot.py` emits each live ficha as a feed card (`stats_ficha`). It removes:
  - the legacy "Pulso Isapre" card;
  - the loose cartera, regional cartera, suscripciones and movilidad cards whose release the ficha covers: monthly cards with a period ≤ the ficha's data period, and month-less yearly books published on or before the ficha's release (the #84 rule). A newer release stays visible until the ficha updates.
- `web/stats-ficha.js` renders the ficha with a "Compartir informe" action. That action shares `data/fichas/<slug>.html` (OG preview, `twitter:card` summary), which redirects to `index.html?abrir=1#sig-…` and opens the ficha detail. It uses the Web Share API, falls back to copying the link, and sends no email.

## Hypotheses and known facts (Dirección 13-nov-2026, applies to all Alicanto)

- A hypothesis is checked against evidence before it is published. If it is confirmed, the ficha states it as a fact
  with its source; if not, the ficha states only what the data shows. `verify_ficha` rejects parenthetical guesses
  such as «(posible cambio de decreto GES)» (`unverified_hypothesis`).
- Public facts of the health system are known, not guessed: e.g. the GES 2025–2028 decree (Decreto N° 29 de 2025,
  Minsal) took effect on 1-dic-2025 and added problems 88, 89 and 90; the GES list only grows, every three years.
- GES problems are named «#. Nombre» (e.g. «7. Diabetes tipo 2»): charts use the short name, tables the official one.
