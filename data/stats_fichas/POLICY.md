# Statistics fichas: publication policy

How to build a ficha, and where each design and truth rule is enforced, is in [PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md) (in Spanish).

## Decision

- **Date:** 28-oct-2026
- **Decided by:** Dirección, approving the proposal "Fichas estadísticas por familia"
- **Decisions:**
  1. **One live ficha per family.** It is replaced each period, and the previous one goes to `archive/`. It replaces the loose SIS minimal cards of the series that belong to the family.
  2. **Auto-publish on PASS.** PASS means deterministic verification plus reconciliation. On FAIL the ficha goes to HOLD.
  3. **The LLM writes only the "Lectura Alicanto".** It is limited to 60 words. With no budget, or when the verifier fails, the block is omitted and the ficha still publishes.
     - **Figure rule, changed 28-oct-2026 15:25:** "Lectura Alicanto puede tener cifras, solo que no caiga en ser redundantes, debe aportar algo más." This replaces the no-numbers rule of the 28-oct review.
  4. **Build order.** F1 Cartera, then F2 Financieras, then F4 GES, then F3 Licencias.

## Scope

- **Covered:** the families marked `"status": "active"` in `config/stats_fichas_v1.json`: F1 Cartera, F4 Casos GES and F6 Prestadores (Boletín IP).
  - Files: `data/stats_fichas/live/<slug>.json`, generated by `scripts/run_stats_fichas.py` from `src/radar_salud/stats_fichas.py` and `src/radar_salud/stats_ficha_<family>.py`.
  - F4 and F6 documents are captured by `src/radar_salud/stats_capture.py` (run by `scripts/run_stats_fichas.py`; `--no-capture` skips it) into `data/stats_fichas/captures/` (URL, SHA-256, parsed values; never the binary). Each listed SIS document is downloaded once, at most 6 per kind and run, newest release first; failures are retried 3 times, then weekly; a file that downloads but fails to parse is recorded (`parse_failed`) and retried after 7 days. Fetches go only to `www.superdesalud.gob.cl` / `superdesalud.gob.cl` over HTTPS (redirects to any other host are blocked), honour robots.txt (shared `datos_gob_sources.load_robots_policy`: 401/403/429/5xx/network fail closed, 404 allowed), stop past 25 MB per response, use a 30 s request timeout and an 8-minute budget for the whole step.
- **Not covered:** newsletter, payments, other AI features, and radar candidates. They still follow CLAUDE.md item 3.
  - A planned family is not activated by this policy. Activating it requires its builder, tests and a PR to staging.
- **F2 (FEFI):** no auto-publication while `HOLD_COVERAGE_ESENCIAL` is active (see `config/editorial_rules_v3.json`).
  - Status: `planned`. The builder is `src/radar_salud/stats_ficha_f2.py`, and capture is `src/radar_salud/stats_capture_fefi.py`, which runs only with `--family F2`.
  - The F2 ficha HOLDs until a person reviews `families.F2.parser_template`. The steps are in PLAYBOOK.md §7.

## Conditions to auto-publish (all are required)

1. **Reconciliation PASS**, recorded in `reconciliation` in the evidence package. For F1 that means:
   - the 10 official codes are present each month;
   - cotizantes + cargas = beneficiarios for every Isapre;
   - the sum across Isapres equals the declared "Total Sistema";
   - the months are contiguous;
   - the declared period equals the last month in the file;
   - the release has a single hash.
   For **F4 GES**:
   - the corrected parser (`ges_pinned_year_reconciled_v2`): the sheet of the reporting year is pinned and the problem rows add up to the declared total, Fonasa and Isapre;
   - the measure of the pinned sheet (year-to-date or quarter) is established only by a reviewer in the registry (`F4.measure.confirmed`, `confirmed_by`); without it HOLD (`measure_unconfirmed`), whatever the header says. The header can only veto: "desde 2005", a different measure ("acumulado del trimestre" is a quarter) or both wordings keep the HOLD;
   - year-to-date cuts never decrease within a year, and the problem list does not change within a year;
   - the quarter is `cut(t) − cut(t−1)` of the same year (Q1 = the March cut), and it is derivable for the latest release.
   For **F6 Boletín IP**:
   - one PDF per topic (acreditación, mediación, reclamos, RNPI), whose file names carry the release's own period. A release page that links another release's PDFs (the "Septiembre 2026" page, 28 oct) is rejected, never relabelled;
   - every figure comes from the independent reading (`config/statistical_analysis_verified_v1.json`) and is found in the text of the captured, hashed PDF, on a page the reading cites (`pages_1based`) and next to a context word of its metric ("acreditad", "trámite", "mediador", "reclamo", "títulos"…);
   - privadas + públicas = solicitudes en tramitación; con observaciones ≤ acreditados; the recomputed claims change rounds to the change the SIS reports.
2. **Deterministic verification PASS** (`verify_ficha`):
   - every number shown in the headline, dek, KPIs, charts, tables, facts, watch items and notes traces to the package;
   - every chart row points to a package value;
   - every source has an https URL and a SHA-256;
   - headline ≤ 140 characters, dek ≤ 40 words, 3–5 KPIs, 2 charts.
3. **A trigger fires** (`evaluate_triggers`):
   - a new period or release;
   - a revision of more than 0.5%;
   - a late release, which adds an item to "Qué mirar".
   - A threshold crossing marks the ficha as featured but does not by itself republish.

## F4 and F6 rules

- **F4 rates** are "tasa bruta": cases of an insurer / mean beneficiaries of the same insurer over the quarter's three month-end stocks × 1.000. The Isapre denominator is the F1 capture. Fonasa beneficiaries are not captured, so the Fonasa rate is "tasa no disponible"; it is never estimated.
- **F4 decree changes** are marked from the data: a problem present in only one of the two quarters compared is flagged, kept out of the ranking, and the total change is also shown for the comparable problems only. Official decree notes go in `F4.decree_changes` only with a source.
- **F4 top problems:** a year-on-year change of 15 % or more, over a base of at least 500 cases in the same quarter one year earlier.
- **F4 supersedes** the loose GES card (`stat_families: ges`) only while the F4 ficha is live in PASS; until then that card stays in HOLD.
- **F6 stocks** (acreditados, solicitudes en tramitación, mediadores, títulos) are never summed across releases. Quarter-on-quarter comparisons need two verified releases and stay "pendiente" otherwise. RNPI counts titles, not unique professionals.

## HOLD (never publishes)

- **Triggers:**
  - reconciliation failure;
  - missing hash;
  - a period older than the live ficha;
  - a deterministic verification FAIL.
- **What happens:**
  - The live ficha stays as it is.
  - The attempt is written to `hold/<slug>-<period>.json` for the coordinator.

## Lectura Alicanto (model)

- **Budget:**
  - one call with the fast route (`luna`) per new release;
  - worst case ≤ USD 0.05 per run;
  - the shared counters in `ai_budget`, the paced monthly budget (`paced_budget_usd`) and the weekly cap enforced by `openai_runtime.call_json`.
- **Verifier** (`verify_lectura`, parser in `src/radar_salud/lectura_verifier.py`). Third independent review, 28-oct, closed by class:
  - **Insurers.** Names, aliases and misspellings are found first (exact names; `CB`, `BMI` only in upper case; then edit distance ≤ 2 on names longer than 5 letters with the same first letter, common words excluded) and masked before any number is read, so "Vida 3" and "Vida Tres" are one insurer and the "3" is never a figure. Fonasa includes "seguro público" and "aseguradora estatal". Any insurer outside the package fails (`insurer_not_in_package`), and one in the package but not in "Qué muestran los datos" fails too (`insurer_not_in_facts`).
  - **Parse.** Each figure becomes (value, decimals, comparator, sign, unit, subject, period). The unit is the metric noun after the figure, or before it in the same clause ("los cotizantes subieron en 1.296"); "N … por cada …" is a ratio; "7 Isapres" is a count.
  - **Scope.** Every `lectura_pool`, `derived_ratios` and `ranks` entry stores `scope` (`system` / `open` / `closed` / `insurer:<code>`). A figure is credited to the insurer or group phrase ("el sistema", "las Isapres", "las abiertas", "las cerradas") that the clause names; with none named, to the system. A system figure credited to an insurer fails (`number_entity_mismatch`).
  - **Period.** Every entry stores `period_kind` (`month` / `ytd` / `interval` / `stock_at`). A month named in the clause matches only `month` or `stock_at` entries of that month. `ytd` requires YTD wording ("desde enero", "en lo que va del año", "entre enero y agosto"). `interval` takes no single month.
  - **Comparators.** These are checked against the true value:
    - "más de", "superior a", "sobre": true > stated;
    - "menos de", "inferior a", "bajo": true < stated;
    - these bounds must also be within 10 %, so "más de 1" never passes;
    - "casi": true < stated and within 5 %;
    - "cerca de", "alrededor de", "unos", "aproximadamente": within 5 %.
  - **Number words.** As before, they must equal a `derived_ratios` or `ranks` entry. An ordinal must trace to a rank of the ranking it names (size, decline, growth, streak) and that ranking's period. It is bound to an insurer only through a copula ("Colmena es la segunda").
  - **Adds something.** A sentence with figures must cite a traced id outside D that is not `trivial`; entity counts such as the 7 open Isapres or the 3 that grow are trivial. Every sentence, with or without figures, fails as a restatement when its content-word Jaccard against a displayed sentence is ≥ 0.5 (`restates_shown_text`), even if it carries a new id. A sentence without figures must also name an audience from `implication_terms`, the single audience list used everywhere.
  - **Also rejects** causal wording without a causal note: the earlier list plus "por lo que", "así que", "de modo que", "de manera que", "lo que hizo que", "lo que llevó a", "nace de", "viene de", "proviene de", "en razón de", "por ende", "en consecuencia". It also rejects more than 60 words, and URLs including bare domains (`superdesalud.gob.cl`).
  - After the Lectura is attached, the whole ficha is verified again. A failure drops the Lectura and the ficha still publishes.
- **Second-model review** (`review_lectura`, `with_review`). After the deterministic verifier passes, one reviewer call checks the Lectura against the evidence package and returns PASS/FAIL with the claim it contests.
  - Route: `lectura.review_route`, `luna`, the cheapest route, with 200 output tokens.
  - Budget: the same budget machinery. `max_run_usd` covers writer + reviewer, so a draft that cannot be reviewed is never paid.
  - Limit: at most 1 reviewer call per ficha. A text that already passed review is never reviewed again.
  - FAIL, no budget, a reviewer error, or no model in the run: the Lectura is omitted (`review_fail` / `review_no_budget` / `review_error` / `review_not_run`) and the ficha still publishes.
- **Chart headlines** derive their counts from the package. "tres crecen" comes from `conteo.abiertas_crecen`. A count word that matches no derived count fails `data:chart_headline_count_not_in_evidence`.
- **Prompt** (`lectura_prompt`): lists what is already shown and, separately, the "Material nuevo" (pool entries, ratios and ranks outside D); the Lectura must add an implication, comparison or derived figure from that material and never restate what is shown.
- **No repeat payment:** a paid attempt for the same `release_key` is never repeated automatically. Only `not_attempted` and `no_budget` are retried.

## Web

- `scripts/export_web_snapshot.py` emits each live ficha as a feed card (`stats_ficha`). It removes:
  - the legacy "Pulso Isapre" card;
  - the loose cartera, regional cartera, suscripciones and movilidad cards whose release the ficha covers: monthly cards with a period ≤ the ficha's data period, and month-less yearly books published on or before the ficha's release (the #84 rule). A newer release stays visible until the ficha updates.
- `web/stats-ficha.js` renders the ficha with a "Compartir informe" action. That action shares `data/fichas/<slug>.html` (OG preview, `twitter:card` summary), which redirects to `index.html?abrir=1#sig-…` and opens the ficha detail. It uses the Web Share API, falls back to copying the link, and sends no email.

## Corrections

- A withdrawn finding keeps its text verbatim in `superseded_meta` (`withdrawn_insight_texts`), with `corrected_at`, `reason` and the superseded and correcting parser versions (CLAUDE.md rule 6). The row tag ("Corregido el 28 oct") and the detail note ("Corregido el 28 oct: …") use the same `correction.label`.
