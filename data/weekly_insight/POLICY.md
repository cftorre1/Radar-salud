# Weekly insight: publication policy

## Decision

- **Date:** 27-oct-2026, 22:59 (America/Santiago)
- **Decided by:** Dirección
- **Quote:** "Que se publique solo el insight"
- **Meaning:** the Monday weekly insight (format A) publishes itself when it passes verification,
  with no manual approval step.

## Scope

- **Covered:** the weekly insight only (`data/weekly_insight/editorial_approved.json`, generated by
  `scripts/run_weekly_insight_a.py` / `src/radar_salud/weekly_insight_a.py`).
- **Not covered:** newsletter, payments, other AI features, and radar candidates. Those still follow
  the general safety rule in CLAUDE.md item 3: no automatic publication.

## Conditions to auto-publish (all are required)

1. **Deterministic verification PASS** (`verify_candidate`): presentation-A rules 1–6. Every number
   in every displayed field must trace to the evidence package (rule 3), and normative IDs must be
   explained in plain words (rule 5). Sources must come from the evidence only.
2. **Independent second-model review PASS**: the reviewer is a different model from the writer,
   returns `verdict: PASS` and reports no `blocker` issues.
3. **Freshness gate**: the insight is anchored on a Monday and expires the next Monday.

## FAIL never publishes

- On a deterministic FAIL, a reviewer FAIL or an error, `editorial_approved.json` is left as it is.
  The old insight then expires under the Monday rule.
- The attempt is saved to `candidates/<monday>-candidate.json` for the coordinator.
- A paid FAIL is not retried automatically for the same Monday (`--force` is required). This
  prevents paying twice.
