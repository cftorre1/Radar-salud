# Weekly insight: publication policy

## Decision

- **Date:** 27-oct-2026, 22:59 (America/Santiago)
- **Decided by:** Dirección
- **Quote:** "Que se publique solo el insight"
- **Meaning:** the Monday weekly insight (format A) publishes itself when it passes verification,
  with no manual approval step.

## Scope

- **Covered:** the weekly insight only (`data/weekly_insight/editorial_approved.json`, generated by
  `scripts/run_weekly_insight_a.py` / `src/radar_salud/weekly_insight_a.py`).
- **Not covered:** newsletter, payments, other AI features, and radar candidates. Those still follow
  the general safety rule in CLAUDE.md item 3: no automatic publication.

## Conditions to auto-publish (all are required)

1. **Deterministic verification PASS** (`verify_candidate`): the presentation rules of the insight's format
   (v2 essay from 16-nov-2026, see below; v1 rules 1–6 for the archive). Every number in every displayed field
   must trace to the evidence package, every quote must be literal in it, and normative IDs must be explained in
   plain words. Sources must come from the evidence only.
2. **Independent second-model review PASS**: the reviewer is a different model from the writer,
   returns `verdict: PASS` and reports no `blocker` issues.
3. **Freshness gate**: the insight is anchored on a Monday and expires the next Monday.

## FAIL never publishes

- On a deterministic FAIL, a reviewer FAIL or an error, `editorial_approved.json` is left as it is.
  The old insight then expires under the Monday rule.
- The attempt is saved to `candidates/<monday>-candidate.json` for the coordinator.
- A paid FAIL is not retried automatically for the same Monday (`--force` is required). This
  prevents paying twice.

## Ficha vs. Insight: stats-release anchor (Dirección, 2-nov-2026)

The product rule, the selection rule and the contract live in
[`data/stats_fichas/PLAYBOOK.md` §9](../stats_fichas/PLAYBOOK.md) (moved there when #117 merged). In short:

- A stats-family ficha whose new period Alicanto **first verified** in the 7 days before the Monday, with a SIS
  publication at most 30 days old on that Monday, is the primary candidate. The selection reason records both dates.
- The Insight must not repeat the ficha. It must cross it with ≥ 2 other sources and pass `contract_check`.
- If the anchored draft fails the contract, the pipeline writes a second draft on the normal topic inside the same
  $1 run cap (`MAX_RUN_USD`) and monthly budget. If that draft does not fit the cap, nothing is paid and the
  attempt stays as a candidate.

## Editorial sessions: brief and contract check are mandatory (Dirección, 2-nov-2026)

Most Insights are written by hand in scheduled editorial sessions, not by the workflow. When the Monday is
anchored on a stats ficha, the session **must** follow these steps. CI enforces them.

1. **Get the brief.** Run `python scripts/run_weekly_insight_a.py --offline --as-of <monday>`. If a stats anchor
   qualifies, it writes `data/weekly_insight/candidates/<monday>-stats-brief.json`. The brief holds the anchor
   with both dates, the cross-references with links and why each is relevant, the allowed anchor figures, the
   ficha text not to repeat, the contract, and a `brief_id`. If no brief is written, the Monday is not anchored:
   write on the normal topic and skip steps 3–4.
2. **Write the Insight** from the brief in `editorial_approved.json` (format A). Declare
   `weekly_insight.stats_anchor = {"ficha_id": …, "brief_id": …}`, as the brief's `insight_must_declare` shows.
3. **Run the contract check** as the last edit:
   `python scripts/check_weekly_insight_contract.py --brief data/weekly_insight/candidates/<monday>-stats-brief.json --write`.
   This writes `data/weekly_insight/contract_checks/<insight id>.json`, which holds the brief id, the check result,
   the Insight's sha256 and the source sha. Exit code 1 means FAIL: fix the draft and rerun. If the Insight cannot
   pass, write it on the normal topic instead (decision 2), and drop the `stats_anchor` field.
4. **Commit together** the Insight, the brief and the record, in the editorial PR toward `staging`.

**The gate.** `tests/test_weekly_insight_stats_anchor.py::test_committed_weekly_insights_pass_the_contract_gate`
runs in the PR QA workflow (full `pytest`) and in the weekly-insight guards. It calls `contract_gate`, which you can
also run as `python scripts/check_weekly_insight_contract.py --gate`. For every Insight in `editorial_approved.json`
and `archive/` that is anchored on a stats ficha, it fails unless all of these hold:

- a record exists;
- its verdict is PASS;
- its sha256 matches the committed Insight, so any edit after the check invalidates it;
- the brief exists, and its id matches its frozen contract inputs;
- the contract, rerun on those inputs, still passes.

An Insight counts as anchored when it declares `stats_anchor`, when its `topic_selection` selected the stats rule,
or when its primary `source_url` is a stats ficha's source file. Insights on a normal topic are not blocked, even
when they cite a stats file as a secondary source. The automated pipeline writes the same brief and record when it
publishes an anchored Insight.

## Format v2: news-style essay (Dirección, 10-nov-2026)

The ~250-word block with fixed sections («Qué pasó», «Lectura Alicanto», «Qué mirar») becomes a ~750-word essay
that reads like a news story. Schema `weekly_insight_a_v2`; the code and the field-by-field schema live in
[`src/radar_salud/weekly_insight_essay.py`](../../src/radar_salud/weekly_insight_essay.py). The reference
example is the 9-nov residencias insight in `editorial_approved.json`, migrated from the approved mockup.

- **Cutover.** Every Monday insight anchored on or after **16-nov-2026** must be v2. A v1 Monday insight from that
  date fails `presentation_a_errors` (`schema_v1_retired_use_weekly_insight_a_v2`) and is never served. Archived
  v1 insights keep rendering and verifying with the v1 rules.
- **Structure.** `body` blocks run lead → context → development; `closing` ends the story and folds «Qué mirar»
  into prose (2–3 signals that would confirm or refute the thesis). Narrative h2 subheads are fine; the old section
  labels are rejected. The first block is the lead paragraph.
- **Length.** Essay (paragraphs + quotes + closing) of 600–900 words (warning outside); FAIL under 500 or over 1.000.
  Headline ≤ 140 characters; dek one sentence, ≤ 35 words; no kicker (see «Editorial rules», 11-nov).
- **Charts.** 1–3, each placed once with a `chart` block right after the paragraph that explains it. `kind` is
  `oficial`, `prensa` or `estimacion` (used to verify, never shown as a label); an estimate chart names its
  `estimate_ref` and its title carries the estimate's footnote letter. `source` links its sources with `[nombre](fN)`.
- **Quotes.** Only `quote` blocks, with `source_ref` and an `attribution` that names the source's issuer or outlet,
  the part of its label before « · » (the same name, or its initials: «Fiscalía Nacional Económica» for «FNE · …»). Each quote, and each phrase of 3 or more words
  between quotation marks in any displayed text, must be literal in the `evidence_excerpts` of a source cited by that
  same text (headline, dek and subheads cite none, so they carry no quotes). `verify_candidate` checks each
  excerpt against the quote pool of its source URL: `evidence.excerpts`, and each signal's `raw_title` and
  `public_lead`. Labels, roles and summaries never count. `[…]` may skip words. Prefer official sources.
- **Sources.** `sources[]` have `id` (`f1`…), `type` (`oficial` or `prensa`), `label`, `url`, `role`. In the text,
  `[Fiscalía Nacional Económica](f1)` renders a direct link to the document plus a numbered superscript to the list.
  Every source must be cited at least once, and the source URLs must equal `source_refs`. The web numbers sources
  by first citation in reading order.
- **Estimates.** Each derived figure is an `estimates[]` entry with a deterministic formula. It is marked in the
  text with `[texto](est:id)` (a lettered footnote mark: a, b, c in reading order, each a note in «Notas» with
  its formula) or used by an estimate chart or key-figure card, and listed in
  `method.estimates`. Numbers written as words are read as one value per phrase («dos mil» = 2.000, «treinta y dos»
  = 32, «1,5 millones» = 1.500.000) and checked like digits; a number word left outside a parsed phrase («miles»,
  «decenas») fails as `number_word_unparsed`. A ratio («8 de cada 10», «la mitad», «dos tercios», «tres cuartos»)
  must sit inside an estimate marker or be the `display` of an estimate, and must be within ±5 points of that one
  estimate. Multiples («el doble», «el triple», «se duplicó», «dobló», «quintuplicó», «un par de») and «mitad», with or
  without article, pass only inside an `est:` marker whose estimate backs them (≈ k or ≈ 100·k %, ±10%); elsewhere
  they fail as `number_word_unparsed`. In `evidence_figures`, numbers written as words must all appear together in
  one evidence item.
- **Key figures** («Lo que debes saber en N cifras»): optional, only when it makes the story clearer. 3–5 cards, each
  with `value`, `text`, `universe` (literal in the card, e.g. «505 de los 1.498 hogares del catastro 2021»), `kind`
  and `source_ref`; `estimate_ref` when the kind is `estimacion`. One idea per card, never mixed universes; one
  `wide` card at most. Placed once with a `key_figures` block.
- **Notes** («Notas», visible after the closing): one entry per estimate, «Estimación Alicanto. «display»: formula =
  value». Only when the text uses an estimate.
- **Method** («Cómo hicimos este análisis», collapsed): `estimates_note`, `discarded` (what was not used and why) and `limits`. The numbered source list follows, visible.
  The method says the text «pasó una revisión independiente» only when `independent_review` has `status: final`
  and a `verdict` of PASS (or PASS_WITH_CORRECTIONS with `corrections_applied`). The pipeline writes it on a second-model
  PASS; an editorial session records each review round and sets the verdict after the last one (`status:
  pending_final` until then). Otherwise it says «Cada número del texto se verificó contra el paquete de evidencia».
- **Traceability.** `evidence_figures` holds the evidence figures copied verbatim (each must trace to the evidence
  numbers); every displayed number traces to it or to an estimate.

### Editorial rules (Dirección, 11-nov-2026)

Standing rules for every insight, not only the 9-nov one. Deterministic where marked; the rest is a FAIL criterion of
the independent review (`weekly_insight_a.review_prompt`).

1. **Header.** Only «Insight de la semana · <fecha>» above the headline. No topic kicker: the pipeline drops it from
   model drafts; in an editorial session a `kicker` field fails (`kicker_not_used`, deterministic).
2. **Dek.** One sentence (`dek_more_than_one_sentence`; abbreviations such as «p. ej.», «S.A.» and numbers such as
   «1.056» do not split; a single-letter initial before a period, as in «Plan B.», and a second sentence that starts
   in lowercase are not counted, a known limit), ≤ 35 words (`dek_over_35_words`), deterministic. It adds what
   the headline does not say (the figures that support it) without repeating its words (rule 8); any comparison
   uses equivalent universes (e.g. supply against population, not one fund against the population). It never summarises or strings two news items together; joining two facts
   needs a connection the evidence shows (review).
3. **Byline.** Exactly «Alicanto · Lectura de N min · N fuentes». No «equipo editorial», no count of official
   sources, no verification date, no evidence legend.
4. **No evidence labels.** The text, charts, key figures and the source list never say «Dato oficial», «Oficial» or
   «Prensa». The sentence names who published each figure («según La Tercera»); a press figure without its outlet
   in the sentence fails the review. `type`/`kind` stay in the data for verification only. The renderer never prints
   these labels (web test on the current insight); the wording of the text is a review criterion.
5. **Estimates as footnotes.** Only when there is an estimate: a superscript letter (a, b, c…) next to the figure,
   chart title or card, pointing to «Notas» at the end with the formula. No dashed styles, no legend.
6. **First screen.** On a 390 × 844 phone the lead paragraph starts on the first screen: the header (date, headline,
   dek, byline) must leave it visible (`tests/browser/insight-essay.spec.cjs`, which sets its own clock to the day after
   the anchor because the suite clock is pinned to an older date). Keep headlines short enough for that.
7. **Headline without unproven causality** (Dirección, 11-nov-2026). The headline states facts the evidence shows.
   It never says that one fact causes or «se paga en» another unless a source in the package states that link; two
   facts that happen at the same time are joined with «y», «mientras» or a colon, never with a causal verb. FAIL
   criterion of the independent review. Applied to the 9-nov insight: «…pero la falta de cuidados ya se paga en camas
   de hospital» → «Residencias para mayores: llega el capital privado a una oferta que sigue siendo pequeña».
8. **Headline and dek never repeat each other** (Dirección, 12-nov-2026: «Primer y segundo párrafo son redundantes…
   un título destacado y luego una bajada de un párrafo sin redundancias»). The dek adds what the headline does not say
   (the figures of the analysis); `dek_repeats_headline` fails when two or more of the headline's content words,
   and at least half of them, reappear in the dek. The headline carries the tension that is the news (Dirección, 12-nov-2026: «el título habla de
   más oferta, pero el cuerpo habla de oferta insuficiente… ¿no debiese el título mostrar ese conflicto?»). Applied
   to the 9-nov insight (Dirección, 12-nov-2026: the conflict is the scale of private investment against an ageing
   population, not the patients without networks, whom private residences would hardly serve; independent review:
   «espacio para mucho más» is an unproven inference): «Un fondo de inversión parte con 250 habitaciones para mayores
   en un país donde más de uno de cada cuatro tendría 60 años o más en 2035» + «El fondo apunta a UF 1.500.000 y hasta
   1.000 habitaciones en un plazo de hasta 15 años, mientras hoy el 19,8% de la población ya tiene 60 años o más».
   Dirección 13-nov-2026 replaced it with the «hecho + tensión» form (short headline with its figure, the tension in
   the dek, no word repeated): «Fondo con meta de UF 1,5 millones apuesta por residencias para mayores» + «Parte con 250
   habitaciones cuando el 19,8% de la población ya tiene 60 años o más; en 2035 sería más de uno de cada cuatro».
   Future titles: three candidates (hecho + tensión, consecuencia de negocio, corto con contraste) scored blind by an
   independent reviewer; tie → hecho + tensión.

**Editorial sessions** write v2 directly in `editorial_approved.json` and run, as the last edit:
`verify_candidate(weekly, evidence, monday)` with the session's evidence package (it must contain the literal text
of every excerpt, under the URL of its source), then `python -m pytest -q tests/test_weekly_insight_essay.py
tests/test_weekly_insight_a.py tests/test_weekly_freshness.py` and `node --test tests/web.test.cjs`. The
independent second review and the auto-publish conditions above are unchanged.
